CORE IDENTITY

1HEAVY0 is a research lab dedicated to the forensic examination of technical infrastructure. We treat technology not as a collection of products but as layered, institutional systems that concentrate power, defer maintenance, and fail in predictable patterns. Our work is clinical, architectural, and unflinching. We produce analysis for engineers, researchers, and policymakers who require structural understanding beyond vendor narratives.
